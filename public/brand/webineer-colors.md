## Role and rationale

- Primary navy conveys trust, infrastructure, professionalism, and product stability.
- Secondary teal signals innovation, live interaction, automation, and digital acceleration.
- Accent teal adds energy and motion without turning the system into a generic tech gradient.
- Light surfaces and cool neutrals preserve readability and SaaS clarity in dashboards and marketing pages.

## Usage guidance

- Use navy for the primary brand surfaces, hero blocks, headers, buttons, and app chrome.
- Use teal for highlights, active states, live indicators, CTAs, and interactive emphasis.
- Keep white and light neutral surfaces for content readability and balanced contrast.
- Reserve accent teal for subtle emphasis rather than large, high-coverage fields.
