# Webineer logo usage guide

## Core identity

The Webineer mark uses a geometric W monogram with a platform line beneath it to suggest a staged digital experience. The geometry is intentionally simple and recognizable even at small sizes.

## Approved logo set

- `webineer-logo.svg` — primary logo for light surfaces
- `webineer-logo-light.svg` — for white or pale surfaces
- `webineer-logo-dark.svg` — for dark navy and black surfaces
- `webineer-icon.svg` — standalone icon
- `webineer-icon-light.svg` — light interface icon
- `webineer-icon-dark.svg` — dark interface icon
- `webineer-wordmark.svg` — wordmark-only version

## Minimum clear space

Maintain at least one-quarter of the letter height worth of clear space around the icon and wordmark.

## Minimum display size

- Digital UI: 20 px height minimum for the icon
- Header/logo lockup: 64 px height minimum in app/marketing layouts
- Favicon: 16 px or 32 px with simplified rendering if needed

## Approved backgrounds

- Primary logo: navy wordmark on white or pale surfaces
- Dark logo: white wordmark on navy or black surfaces
- Monochrome variants: use either full navy or full white as needed

## Avoid

- Stretching or skewing the logo
- Adding shadows, gradients, or 3D effects
- Recolouring the icon outside the approved palette
- Using the icon with low-contrast background combinations
- Replacing the icon with the full wordmark in favicon contexts
