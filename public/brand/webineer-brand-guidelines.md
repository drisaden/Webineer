# Webineer brand guidelines

## Identity summary

Webineer is a webinar automation and management platform built for sharp execution, compelling live experiences, and measurable conversion. The identity blends a confident navy foundation with a modern teal signal to communicate intelligence, motion, and reliability.

## Logotype concept

The symbol is a stylized W built from precise, rising diagonal strokes. The lower platform line references the idea of a well-constructed content stage: organized, engineered, and ready for presentation.

## Typography system

Webineer uses exactly two typeface families:

- Primary display typeface: Michroma
  - Used for hero headings, major brand statements, technical labels, and selected micro-accents.
  - Futuristic, geometric, and architectural without becoming difficult to read.
- Secondary typeface: Outfit
  - Used for body text, navigation, dashboard labels, buttons, documentation, and marketing copy.
  - Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold, 700 Bold.

### Hierarchy

1. Major brand headings: Michroma, selectively used.
2. Section headings: Outfit SemiBold.
3. Body text: Outfit Regular.
4. Navigation and interface labels: Outfit Medium.
5. Small technical labels: Michroma, used sparingly.

### Restrictions

- No third font family is introduced.
- Michroma is reserved for short display moments and technical labels, not long-form paragraphs.
- Outfit carries the interface and reading experience across the product.
- Letter spacing is restrained; it is not applied across all text.
- Headings remain readable and do not become all-caps by default.

## Palette

## Recommended applications

- SaaS dashboard: icon + navy header surface
- Website navigation: white or pale background with navy wordmark and teal highlights
- Browser favicon: icon only on square navy background
- Email header: navy or white lockup with teal accents
- Social avatar: navy square icon with teal W
